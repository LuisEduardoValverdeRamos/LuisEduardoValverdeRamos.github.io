I am a political scientist trained at the Pontificia Universidad Católica del Perú (PUCP). I graduated first in my class, and my licentiate thesis received a distinction (*mención sobresaliente*) in 2025. I studied on a Beca 18 scholarship, Peru's national merit award, and spent an exchange semester at Ghent University (Belgium) with a DARI scholarship. I am from Pedregal Grande (Catacaos, Piura) and work from Lima.

I am currently a researcher at the Electoral and Governance School (ESEG) of Peru's National Elections Jury (JNE), a consultant to the Corruption Observatory of the Lima Bar Association, a teaching assistant in Political Economy at PUCP and Director General of the Peruvian National Schools Debate Team.

## Consulting

I advise public, private and civil-society organisations on applied research, using mixed methods, both quantitative and qualitative: what can be measured with the information available, how to design an instrument, or how to read a set of records. I have worked with the Corruption Observatory of the Lima Bar Association (assessing public procurement indicators, since 2026); with ProDiálogo for the JNE (technical team for the report on the Peruvian voter, 2026); with the LPDE (the League's first report on the school debate ecosystem, covering 70 institutions, and seven policy briefs, 2026); and with Roche Pharma Peru through Sapienza Consultora (administrative health data, 2025).

I work in person in Lima and online with the rest of Peru and Latin America.

## Debate

- Director General of the Peruvian National Schools Debate Team. Before that I was part of its coaching staff: in Panama in 2026 the team won its fourth consecutive title at the *Mundial de Debate Escolar en Español* (MED), the world schools championship held in Spanish; the team I coached reached the quarterfinals and placed among the tournament's top 10 speakers.
- Co-head coach of the PUCP Debate Society and debate coach at Colegio Alpamayo, both since 2026.
- Since 2024, I have coached university debaters from the Dominican Republic online: in 2025 I prepared the first all-Dominican pair to reach the round of 16 at CMUDE, the world universities debating championship held in Spanish, and, in 2026, the pairs that reached the quarterfinals and the round of 16 (CMUDE Ecuador).
- As a debater: national inter-university champion (CNDI 2025), semifinalist at CMUDE Madrid 2022, quarterfinalist at CMUDE Panama 2023 and runner-up at the Torneo Rosarista (Bogotá, 2023). As an adjudicator: I have served on the adjudication teams of school and university tournaments in Costa Rica, Ecuador, Peru and Venezuela; judge of the CMUDE 2026 grand final (Ecuador) and chief adjudicator of TNDE 2025.
