*I grew up in Pedregal Grande, a small rural community outside Catacaos in northern Peru. That origin is part of why I do what I do.*

Hello! I am **Luis Valverde Ramos**, a Political Scientist from Catacaos, Piura, graduated with distinction from the **Pontificia Universidad Católica del Perú (PUCP)**, with an exchange semester at **Ghent University**, Belgium. I combine political science training with advanced quantitative analysis, large-scale data processing, and evidence production for public policy and communication strategy — collaborating with government agencies, research centers, and the private sector across multidisciplinary teams.

### Beyond My Work

I am an active participant in Peru's competitive debate community — **National Interuniversity Debate Champion (2025)** and Semifinalist at the **Campeonato Mundial Universitario de Debate en Español** in Madrid (2022). I currently coach the Peruvian national school debate team ahead of the **Mundial de Debate Escolar** in Panama, and train university debaters from Peru and the Dominican Republic in British Parliamentary format. I believe that communicating well is as important as thinking well. Outside of work, I practice Muay Thai.

### Current Interests

I am self-driven in my learning — currently deepening my skills in applied statistics and machine learning, exploring macroeconomics and human behavior as frameworks for understanding why people and institutions act the way they do. I also bring these interests into my debate coaching, helping students engage with data and evidence-based reasoning as part of their argumentative practice.
