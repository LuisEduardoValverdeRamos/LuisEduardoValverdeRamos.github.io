The model does not aim to explain stalling causally; it ranks risk — identifying which works resemble those that stalled before. That distinction matters, because the policy question is not "what causes stalling?" but "where should we look first?".

The work belongs to the Q-LAB team; what is published on the project site is the citable reference.
