The result is a set of voter archetypes the institution can use to decide whom each piece of civic education addresses. The official solution is the one included in the institutional report published in 2026; parallel methodological explorations do not support it and are not cited.

Authorship of the report is institutional: what is described here is the method, not a claim over the product.
